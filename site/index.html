Mantel

A console-style home screen for Windows handhelds.

Every game you have installed, from Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox / Game Pass, Ubisoft Connect and anything else, on one fullscreen shelf you drive with the controller. Games still launch through their own stores, so nothing gets reinstalled.

Features

A home screen, not another launcher to manage

Big art, one shelf of games and a controller in your hands. Mantel finds your games, keeps time and gets out of the way when you play.

Works on first launch

Mantel finds installed games on its own: no plugins, no per-store setup.

Controller first

Every screen works with the gamepad, the touchscreen or a keyboard. The control center (≡) closes games, restarts or powers off the device.

Playtime that adds up

Mantel times every session itself and, with your accounts connected, brings in playtime from Steam and Xbox across all your devices.

Your whole collection

Sign in to Steam or Xbox to also see the games you own but haven't installed. Picking one opens its install page in Steam or the Xbox app.

Steps aside while you play

Mantel minimizes when a game starts and comes back when it exits.

Art for everything

Covers, backgrounds and logos come from Steam and GOG; a free SteamGridDB key covers the rest.

Accounts and privacy

No servers. No telemetry.

Mantel talks only to the services you use. Signing in is optional: it adds the games you own and your playtime from other devices.

Steam

You sign in on Steam's own page inside Mantel; Mantel never sees your password, and email Steam Guard codes work. Mantel then reads your owned games with the same short-lived token the Steam store page uses.

Xbox

You sign in on Microsoft's own page and allow Mantel to read your Xbox profile. Mantel reads your play history and minutes played from Xbox Live.

Artwork

Steam's store and image servers, GOG's public API and, if you add a key, SteamGridDB.

Stored on your PC

Everything stays in %APPDATA%\com.playmantel.launcher\. user.json holds settings, favorites, playtime and the account tokens; cache.json can be deleted at any time.

Signing out

Settings → Accounts signs you out and deletes the tokens.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that expected?

Yes, for now. That's Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, and it warns about installers that aren't code-signed. Mantel's installer isn't code-signed yet, so choose More info → Run anyway.

Every release is built by GitHub Actions from the public source code, so you can see exactly what goes into it.

How is it different from Playnite?

Playnite is a mature, open-source library manager with a big plugin ecosystem and both a desktop and a fullscreen mode; you can make it do almost anything. Mantel is narrower on purpose: one controller-first home screen that finds games from the supported stores on first launch, with no plugins and no per-store setup.

If you love tuning your setup, Playnite is great. If you want a handheld that boots straight into your games, try Mantel.

How is it different from the Xbox full screen experience on the ROG Xbox Ally?

The Xbox full screen experience is Microsoft's console-style shell for Windows handhelds, built around the Xbox app. Mantel is an independent, open-source app that treats every store the same, shows the games and playtime from your Steam and Xbox accounts side by side, and launches each game through its own store.

It's a regular Windows app, so you can try it without changing how your handheld boots, and turn on Start with Windows when you want it as your home screen.

Is it free?

Yes. Mantel is free and open source under the GPL-3.0-or-later license, with no ads, no subscription and no account required. The source code is on GitHub.

Which handhelds does it run on?

Mantel is built on and for the ROG Xbox Ally X, and it should feel at home on any Windows handheld or a TV PC. The installer is for 64-bit (x64) Windows. If something looks off on your device, open an issue.

Do I have to reinstall or move my games?

No. Mantel reads what your stores have already installed and launches each game through its own store, so nothing gets reinstalled or reconfigured.

Does Mantel collect any data?

No. Mantel has no servers and sends no telemetry; your settings, playtime and account tokens stay on your PC. The privacy statement lists every service it talks to.
